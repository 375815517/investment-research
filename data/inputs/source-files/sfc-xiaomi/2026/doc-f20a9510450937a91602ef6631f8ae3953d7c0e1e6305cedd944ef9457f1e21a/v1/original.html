Remarks:

1.  In the case of parallel trading of a stock at two stock counters, the short positions for the stock at the two counters will be aggregated and shown in the original counter.  For details, please refer to the FAQs (question B8).

2. The aggregated reportable short positions published on this page are calculated based on information contained in notifications submitted to the SFC by persons who have reportable short positions or their reporting agents on a reporting day. A reportable position is a net short position that as at the close of the last trading day of each week,

a. amounts to or exceeds the threshold of 0.02% of the market capitalization of the listed issuer concerned, or HK$30 million, whichever is lower, for all Designated Securities except collective investment schemes (CISs) which include Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) and Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs); or

b. amounts to or exceeds the threshold of HK$30 million for CISs.

3. "n.a." represents "not available" because the closing price of the specified share is not available on the reporting day.
